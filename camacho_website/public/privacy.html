1. Information we collect

When you visit our website

Our website is primarily informational. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse it. If you contact us using the phone number or email address listed on the site, we will receive whatever information you choose to share with us (such as your name, phone number, email, and the contents of your message). Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and basic analytics.

When you visit our recycling centers

When you redeem California Redemption Value (CRV) beverage containers with us, we record transaction information required to operate our business and comply with state recycling regulations. This may include the materials and weights recycled, the amount paid, the date and location, and — where required — your name and signature confirming the transaction (see Section 4).

When our staff use the Daily Log App

The Daily Log App is an internal tool used by authorized Camacho Recycling staff to record daily operations. Accounts are issued by an administrator. Through the App we collect:

  • Account information — the email address and account identifier used to sign in.
  • Precise location — used to automatically select the nearest certified recycling site when a staff member starts a log sheet (see Section 3).
  • Operational content — log-sheet entries such as material types, weights, and totals, plus the customer name and signature captured for a transaction (see Section 4).

The App does not access your camera or photo library, does not include advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate our recycling centers and process CRV redemption transactions;
  • Pay recyclers accurately and keep records of transactions;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our website and the Daily Log App;
  • Authenticate staff and secure access to internal tools;
  • Automatically identify the nearest certified site for staff creating log sheets;
  • Respond to your questions and requests;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and recycling-program reporting obligations (for example, records associated with a certified recycling location); and
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of Camacho Recycling, our customers, and the public.

3. Location data

The Daily Log App requests device location only while the App is in use, and only from authorized staff devices. Location is used to automatically select the nearest certified recycling site so the correct site name, address, and certification number are attached to a log sheet. Staff can decline the location permission and select a site manually; location is a convenience, not a requirement to use the App. We do not use this location data for advertising and do not sell it.

4. Customer names & signatures

When you recycle with us, we may collect your name and an on-screen signature as a record that a transaction took place and that payment was received. This information is collected for recordkeeping, fraud prevention, and compliance with recycling-program requirements. We retain these records for as long as required by law and our business needs, and we do not sell them or use them for marketing.

4a. Text message (SMS) & email receipts

At the end of a transaction you may choose to receive your receipt by text message or email. If you enter your mobile number, you agree to receive one transactional text message per transaction from Camacho Recycling containing a link to your receipt. We do not send marketing messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Your phone number and email address are used only to deliver your receipt. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of recycling with us, and message frequency varies with your transactions (typically one message per visit).

You opt in verbally at our counter when a staff member asks whether you would like your receipt by text and you provide your mobile number and confirm. Full program terms are in our Terms & Conditions.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted vendors who help us operate our business and technology, such as our website host, our cloud/database provider (Google Firebase), which stores App data on our behalf, and our messaging providers (Twilio and SendGrid), which deliver receipts you request by text or email, each under their security and privacy commitments.
  • Legal & regulatory — government agencies and recycling programs (such as California's CRV program) where reporting or records are required, and to comply with applicable law, subpoenas, or lawful requests.
  • Business protection — to enforce our terms, prevent fraud, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Camacho Recycling and others.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to satisfy legal, accounting, tax, and recycling-program recordkeeping requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. App data is protected by authenticated sign-in and stored with our cloud provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights & choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what personal information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion, subject to our legal and recordkeeping obligations. To make a request, use the contact details in Section 12. On your mobile device, you can also control or revoke the App's location permission at any time through your device settings.

9. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any of your rights, contact us using the information below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

10. Children's privacy

Our website, services, and App are intended for a general and business audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here, and your continued use of our website, services, or App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
